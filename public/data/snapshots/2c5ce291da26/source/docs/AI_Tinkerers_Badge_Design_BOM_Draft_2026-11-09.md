# AI Tinkerers Badge: First-Draft Design BOM

This is a component-selection working document, not a schematic-derived manufacturing BOM or an instruction to purchase. Quantities are planning estimates per badge. Routine resistors, capacitors, inductors and similar support parts are omitted as requested; their required functions still appear in engineering notes. No PCB footprints or electrical substitutes are approved merely by appearing here.

Basis: *AI_Tinkerers_Badge_Project_Spec.md*, version 0.2, 8 November 2026; *AI_Tinkerers_Badge_Prior_Art_2026-11-07.md*; current supplier/manufacturer checks; and the later decisions to include **both Qwiic and SAO** and use **eight WS2812C-2020-V6 addressable RGB LEDs** with direct ESP32-S3 data, plus **BH1730FVC ambient sensing** and the **separate red/green power-status indicator** defined below and in `spec.md` version 0.6. The automatic sleep/wake baseline supersedes the populated everyday power slide switch; dock and USB presence must be sensed separately. IR remains a working option; an active NFC reader remains deferred. Exact battery capacity, panel purchase SKU, LED optics, GPIO mapping, batch size and budget are open.

## Current schematic choices

The [9 November schematic implementation record](design/implementation_status.md) is the current electrical build list and overrides conflicting candidate rows below. It includes the two-wire battery and separate DNP sensor interface, charging inhibit, revised rail arrangement, SMF6.0A input TVS, isolated ADC path, display buffer and status expander. The historical sourcing ledger remains useful evidence; it does not approve substitutions or purchases. Full support-component assignments are in [project pinouts](pinouts.md).

## Reading the tables

- **Working** means the preferred starting candidate for this draft, not production approval.
- **Option** is not populated unless chosen. **Reference only** is useful precedent that needs replacement or qualification.
- Quantities such as `0/1` mean absent by default and one if the option is selected. Alternative MPNs replace the primary row; do not add both automatically.
- A **C-number** identifies a supplier/catalog record. LCSC inventory and JLC assembly inventory are **separate checks**. An LCSC listing alone does not establish JLC assembly support.
- **E/S** means the JLC listing permits Economic and Standard PCBA; **S-only** means Standard PCBA only. Basic/Extended is a separate catalog classification.
- Live quantities are point-in-time observations, not reserved parts. The procurement ledger records exactly which parts had live stock verified. `Unverified` does not mean out of stock.
- This is not a costed BOM: no guessed prices or total build cost are supplied. Later costing must separate components, PCB/assembly, display, battery, enclosure, dock, shipping and taxes.

## 1. Power architecture and main parts

Working chain:

`USB-C 5 V + dock 5 V → separately protected inputs → reverse-blocking source selection → 1S charger with power path → nominal 3.3 V buck-boost → badge loads`

The battery connects to the charger's battery port, not directly to either input. USB and dock supplies must not feed each other. Each badge has its own charger; the dock distributes protected power, not a parallel battery connection. The baseline keeps the MCU supply available for automatic deep-sleep wake and supports operation while charging. Charger limits, NTC temperature protection, and pack protection work without successful firmware initialization.

### Automatic sleep, wake and service contract

`spec.md` defines the operating states: event operation, externally powered operation/charging, bounded housekeeping, and storage deep sleep. **No dedicated everyday power switch is fitted.** PWR-11/PWR-12 remain unpopulated options; UI-03's existing buttons provide wake. The present PWR-05 charger and PWR-06 regulator remain the working choices, subject to electrical and current measurements.

- Finish or safely defer pending writes and save state before sleep. For a confirmed loaner return, clear local attendee data and the persistent display before reporting cleanup complete. Dock presence starts only the defined return workflow; USB power never implies checkout. Event-end/inactivity triggers and timeouts remain to be specified.
- Stop Wi-Fi and BLE, including advertising/scanning, for storage deep sleep. Blank PWR-13 and all eight RGB pixels; put the display, ambient sensor, NFC MCU interface, optional IR, and accessories into reviewed low-power states. Preserve only required wake/retention circuitry. Motion wake is optional during events and disabled in storage/transport. E-paper retention and passive NFC reading do not require an awake MCU.
- Route at least one existing UI-03 button to a confirmed ESP32-S3 deep-sleep wake input. If USB/dock insertion wake is included, PWR-14 must provide a safe signal with validated polarity, retention and wake behavior, including when power is already present. Do not rely on USB data, ordinary interrupts, or a powered-down GPIO expander to wake the MCU. A continuously asserted source-present level must not cause a sleep/wake loop. The final pin/circuit allocation is unresolved.
- Keep the MCU supply available in baseline deep sleep. Do not turn off PWR-06 and expect the MCU to restore its own supply. A future rail-off/ship-mode design needs an independent hardware wake path and reviewed charging behavior. Charger/NTC/pack protection must remain autonomous during sleep, reset and firmware failure.
- Keep independent EN/reset and BOOT access in MCU-03. Provide service access for a full power cycle using the battery connector after disconnecting USB, dock and any back-powering accessories; reset is not battery isolation. No extra everyday button or power-management IC is selected.

**Measurements and release gate:** Measure complete-badge current for event use, idle, housekeeping and deep sleep, with USB only, dock only, both inputs and no external power, plus representative accessories. Record input/system load separately from battery charging or discharge current. Account for charger/regulator quiescent current, protected-BAT sensing, NFC, sensors, LED standby, pull-ups, switches and backfeed. Establish the acceptable storage duration/current budget, then decide whether extra PWR-07 gating or deeper hardware off is needed. Test low-battery sleep/restart, wake reliability, accidental motion, stuck input levels, and charging/NTC protection with firmware absent or stalled. No storage-life estimate is established by an MCU-only sleep-current figure.

### Power / low-battery indicator contract

**Package choice:** use PWR-13 as the tiny, stocked working candidate. It is **0603 imperial / 1608 metric**, not 0402 imperial (1.0 × 0.5 mm), and needs a four-pad footprint rather than an ordinary two-pad 0603 LED footprint. Its green is **yellow-green, nominal 572 nm**, not emerald green. A true 0402 two-color alternative has not been qualified; two separate 0402 LEDs are a possible optical/layout alternative, not an additional baseline population. Keep the status light separate from the role/affinity light guides and away from the ambient sensor opening.

**Electrical implementation:** the [Broadcom datasheet, AV02-0584EN, 2022-05-13](https://docs.broadcom.com/doc/AV02-0584EN) gives green anode **pin 1**, green cathode **pin 3**, red anode **pin 2**, red cathode **pin 4**. Tie pins 3 and 4 to GND on the PCB. Connect `PWR_LED_G GPIO → R_G → pin 1` and `PWR_LED_R GPIO → R_R → pin 2`. This makes a board-level common-cathode arrangement; each output sources current from the MCU's regulated nominal 3.3 V domain. No raw BAT, charger SYS, USB 5 V, level shifter or LED-driver IC connects to these LED pins. Use two non-strapping output-capable pins, with external weak pulldowns and firmware low defaults so reset/high-impedance states are dark. Actual GPIO numbers remain unassigned until the complete pin map closes.

Start bench evaluation with **1.5 kΩ per color**, aiming for roughly **0.5–1 mA** per lit die, then tune resistor values/PWM independently for visibility and color balance. The datasheet's typical Vf at 20 mA is 1.95 V red / 2.20 V green, with 2.60 V maximum for each at that test condition; those values do not guarantee low-current brightness. At a 3.6 V maximum rail and 1% 1.5 kΩ resistor, even the conservative zero-LED-drop bound is below 2.43 mA per output. Recalculate using the final rail, GPIO VOH/current budget, resistor tolerance and LED curves. Normally illuminate only one die; both on is not a defined status. Resistors, pulldowns and ADC support passives remain required design notes, **not extra rows in this passives-omitted BOM**.

**Status policy, after firmware initialization:**

| State | Indicator | Meaning / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Badge on, battery operation, valid voltage and low-warning latch clear | Steady dim green | Normal powered operation; not a fuel-percentage or firmware-health guarantee |
| Badge on, battery operation, sustained low battery | Red, green off | Low-battery warning; also show a text/icon cue on e-paper |
| Badge on with valid USB/dock input | Green | Externally powered. Show charging/fault details separately on e-paper; green does not mean fully charged |
| Reset/boot, storage deep sleep, or a future MCU-rail-off mode | Both off | Storage is dark even with external power; autonomous charging may continue without lighting this LED |

Keep green/red stable only through normal on-state idle/light-sleep by allocating suitable GPIO retention and testing it; explicitly blank both before storage deep sleep. If an always-lit indicator before firmware starts, or charge indication while the MCU is off, becomes a requirement, it needs a separately reviewed hardware path. Do not wire charger `CHG` directly to the red die: it means charging, not low battery. Read charger `CHG`/`PGOOD` as status inputs with pull-ups to the MCU domain and correct power-off behavior. These combined-input status signals do not establish which source is present; use PWR-14 for USB/dock distinction. [TI BQ24074 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24074.pdf).

**Low-battery detection:** reuse the existing battery-ADC baseline. Sense the protected pack/BAT node, not regulated 3V3 or charger SYS. Prefer an ADC1-capable input, a divider with tolerance margin below the selected ADC range at maximum charge voltage, an input filter, adequate settling time and calibrated conversion. The sensing path must not inject battery current into an unpowered MCU or bypass battery isolation: qualify divider gating/isolation, leakage and discharge behavior before schematic release, and add any active gating part to the later BOM if required. No fuel gauge or comparator is added solely for this LED. [Espressif ADC oneshot/calibration guidance](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/stable/esp32s3/api-reference/peripherals/adc/adc_oneshot.html).

Use **below 3.50 V for 10 seconds to enter low**, and **above 3.65 V for 30 seconds to clear**, as provisional battery-mode firmware thresholds, not cell safety limits or state-of-charge percentages. During awake operation, sample about once per second, filter readings and evaluate under a consistent representative load; validate radio/display transient sag and cold/aged-cell behavior. While externally powered, suspend this battery-discharge warning and use charge/status information instead; after unplugging, re-evaluate fresh settled battery readings rather than retaining a charging-voltage estimate. Invalid sensing must not be reported as a healthy battery. Define controlled low-voltage shutdown separately from this early warning, before the selected pack's allowed discharge endpoint; neither the pack protection trip nor the regulator UVLO is the user warning threshold.

**Prototype release checks:** audit the four-pad footprint and orientation; test both colors through the final window at low current; verify boot/reset, idle/light-sleep, dark storage deep sleep and any future rail-off mode, charging during storage, missing/dead battery, USB/dock insertion/removal, threshold hysteresis, ADC calibration and off-state leakage. Reserve two additional GPIOs and include on-state indicator current in the runtime budget. These are documented design choices, not a validated schematic or firmware implementation.

### Power decisions before a schematic freeze

1. Select a documented **4.2 V-charge, single-cell protected pack**, its charge/discharge limits, NTC curve and physical envelope. A protection circuit is not a charger. Do not use a 4.35 V pack or LiFePO4 under a 4.2 V Li-ion assumption.
2. Derive charge current from the actual cell and thermal test. A small wearable enclosure is a poor place to assume the charger's headline maximum current is usable. Hardware must start with safe limits even before firmware runs.
3. USB-C is a 5 V sink here, without a PD requirement. Both CC pins need the correct sink termination even though those resistors are omitted from this draft. A USB-C connector does not grant 1.5 A or 3 A automatically. Choose a conservative input limit or add valid current-advertisement detection, and respect USB data-port power rules.
4. Implement the automatic deep-sleep baseline and measure full-system storage draw. Resolve PWR-14 source sensing, optional insertion wake, button wake, peripheral shutdown and service power-cycle access. Keep charging autonomous during storage; revisit regulator shutdown, battery isolation or ship mode only if the measured drain warrants it.
5. Scope-test radio transmission, e-paper inrush, RGB LED updates/full-white load, IR pulses and accessory load together at the low-battery limit. Regulator output-current marketing figures do not guarantee the same current at minimum input voltage or inside the final enclosure.
6. Test USB-only, dock-only, both inputs, either input removed, dead/absent battery, shorted contacts and reverse/misaligned docking. Confirm distinct source-presence reporting, no checkout on USB insertion, reliable wake/sleep transitions, and no backfeed into unplugged inputs or powered-down peripherals.

**Capacity is not selected yet.** An illustrative calculation with a 3.3 V average load, 85% conversion efficiency and 80% usable nominal battery capacity gives roughly **525 mAh for 100 mA average over four hours**, or **1,050 mAh for 200 mA**: `capacity_Ah = 3.3 × average_A × 4 / (3.7 × 0.85 × 0.80)`. These are assumptions, not measured runtime. Radios, LED brightness and SAO accessories can dominate. Prototype around the documented pack candidates, then measure the representative event workload before committing capacity.

The exact V6 part specifies **3.3 V minimum supply** and **VIH = 0.55 × VDD**. Direct 3.3 V GPIO data is retained. A nominal 3.3 V regulator can fall below the LED's minimum after tolerance, distribution loss and transients. This is an accepted **prototype validation boundary**, not a datasheet-compliant guarantee below 3.3 V. Test startup, low battery, Wi-Fi, display activity and full-white steps while measuring LED and MCU rails. Record color/data errors, resets and brownouts. **Support circuitry:** retain local supply decoupling at each pixel, bulk capacitance for the chain and a series damping resistor between the MCU and first DIN, with values/placement finalized from the exact reference circuit and scope tests. **Current, sleep and storage:** the nominal **5 mA per color** gives about **120 mA for eight full-white pixels**, before tolerance and other system loads. The specified **≤1 µA dark standby per pixel** is distinct from illuminated operation; measure actual all-black and storage draw. If switched, prevent DIN backfeed while unpowered, including boot/reset and power sequencing; do not rely on a series resistor alone for isolation.

**Prototype release tests:** brightness through the diffuser, uniformity across the three role LEDs, low-brightness color, camera flicker with the specified **2 kHz PWM**, minimum-rail and cold behavior, data integrity during radio activity, full-white load steps and all-black/off leakage. ## 13. Decisions that block schematic/PCB release

1. **Battery, charging and automatic sleep:** pack, autonomous NTC/protection and safe charge/input limits, regulator headroom, measured storage leakage, button/optional insertion wake, independent reset/BOOT, service power cycle and thermal behavior. Close PWR-14 protected sensing and actual wake pins; measure before adding deeper off. Qualify PWR-13 visibility/footprint, two GPIOs, dark storage, ADC off-state isolation, warning hysteresis and safe low-voltage sleep/restart.
2. **Exact panel and flex:** panel revision, controller, flex fold, connector drawing/pin 1/contact side, and supported waveform/driver. Qualify the display boost MOSFET replacement and actual support circuit.
3. **Addressable-LED qualification:** validate the chosen single-3.3 V prototype against the eight-pixel WS2812C-2020-V6 minimum/tolerance boundary, direct-data margins, brightness/current limits, placement/diffuser tests, low-brightness color/camera flicker, standby/backfeed behavior and JLC assembly sourcing.
4. **Dock identification and source sensing:** choose a data/identity mechanism and contact count; validate independent USB/dock presence with either or both inputs connected. Power pads alone cannot identify returns or guarantee personal-data erasure, and USB insertion must not trigger checkout.
5. **GPIO and I²C map:** allocate actual ESP32-S3 module pins, straps, wake inputs, USB, expansion, test and recovery before calling any candidate final. Address conflicts with arbitrary accessories remain possible.
6. **NFC:** NTAG versus ST25DV, antenna geometry, errata-safe firmware, phone test matrix and public/limited-purpose payload policy. Active reader remains excluded unless its use case is explicit.
7. **IR:** include both transmitter and receiver or deliberately omit the subsystem; validate optical placement, protocol, range and peak current.
8. **Manufacturing:** accept Standard PCBA/X-ray for the selected S3 module and motion sensor, or explicitly redesign. Confirm SAO wave-solder/mixed-process handling, display insertion and battery attachment as separate assembly steps. Recheck live parts and quantities at quote time.
9. **Runtime and fleet behavior:** verify four-hour representative operation, housekeeping and dark deep sleep, no BLE/Wi-Fi during storage, reliable wake, firmware recovery, autonomous charging while asleep, and a confirmed loaner-return flow that clears stored data and persistent e-paper content. Set storage-current/duration targets from prototype measurements.
