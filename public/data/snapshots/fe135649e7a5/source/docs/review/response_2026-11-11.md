## NFC-1: copper under the coil

The reviewer's measurements reproduce. The cause was mine: `sync_board_design_review.py` dropped the old `MECH_NFC` footprint, which carried the plane keepout, and my 10 November review then stated a clearance that the saved board did not have. That statement is corrected in the earlier review.

What changed:

- A rule area named `NFC_WINDOW` on F.Cu, In1.Cu, In2.Cu and B.Cu, x 180.55–200.45, y 165.2–180.0. It extends to the bottom board edge, so no ground bridge closes a loop below the coil. Zone fills are excluded.
- `tinkerbadge.kicad_dru` rejects any track or via inside the area unless it is on `NFC_LA` or `NFC_LB`. The coil and its feed stay legal.
- RGB_LINK_7 and every other unrelated track and via were removed from the window and rerouted. The router (`tools/route_remaining.py`) masks the window on all its layers.
- The coil's inner end is now `LA` and the outer end `LB`. The tag sits just above the window, so `LA` drops straight down on F.Cu to a via at the inner end and `LB` reaches the outer end outside the winding. The only copper in the window is the coil, that one feed and the two terminal vias.

| Measurement | Checkpoint `3120d6e` | Revision 0.3 |
|---|---|---|
| In1.Cu ground in the winding rectangle | 233.21 mm² (99.66 %) | 0 |
| In2.Cu ground in the winding rectangle | 233.21 mm² (99.66 %) | 0 |
| Inner-layer fill in the window, from the exported Gerbers | 6,945 and 6,926 of 7,326 sample points | 0 of 7,326 on both |
| Unrelated tracks or vias in the window | RGB_LINK_7 and others | none |

Two checks now guard this. `tools/check_nfc_window.py` reads the saved board; `tools/check_nfc_gerbers.py` reads the exported Gerbers. `tools/export_jlc.py` runs both and refuses to write a package if either fails. Both fail on the checkpoint files and pass on revision 0.3.

The window margin around the winding is 1.45 mm on the left, 0.8 mm above and 0.45 mm on the right, where D408 sits. Moving D408 would break the even spacing of the affinity lights, so it stays until a read test shows it matters.

Not done: coil impedance, resonance and phone read tests. C507 stays at 27 pF with C508 as the trim position. That starting value was calculated for the coil in free space, about 1.7 µH, which the board now approximates.

## PWR-1: battery temperature window

The BQ24074 fixes the ratio between its hot and cold thresholds, so the window can be moved but not narrowed. With the single 10 kΩ NTC it sat at 3.5–47.2 °C nominal with a hot corner at 51.0 °C.

- RT102, 100 kΩ B3950, is now in parallel with RT101. The pair behaves as a 9.09 kΩ B3950 part. JLCPCB's library had no 8.2 kΩ NTC, the nearest standard value below 10 kΩ, when searched on 11 November.
- Both parts moved from beside the charger to (150.5, 142) and (153.5, 142), opposite the middle of the cell allowance and 32 mm from the charger. The 3.3 V regulator and its inductor are 8–12 mm away, so their heating belongs in the trip-point measurement.

| Trip point | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Hot, nominal | 47.2 °C | 44.7 °C |
| Hot, IC threshold and bias corners | 43.7–51.0 °C | 41.3–48.4 °C |
| Cold, nominal | 3.5 °C | 1.7 °C |
| Cold, lowest corner | 1.8 °C | 0.0 °C |

Same formulas and datasheet limits as the review (bias 72/75/78 µA, hot 270/300/330 mV, cold 2.0/2.1/2.2 V), nominal R25 and beta. The nominal window now sits inside 0–45 °C. The worst hot corner is still 3.4 °C above 45 °C, and pushing it lower would put the cold corner below 0 °C. Closing that gap needs either a pack rated for it or a measured unit-to-unit spread.

Still open, as the review says: choose the exact pack, check its polarity and charge limits, and measure trip temperatures and lag at the cell. Charging should not be left unattended or worn until then.

## MECH-1: SAO socket

`SAO_Socket_2x03_Front` is redrawn from Sullins drawing 11160 revision J: 12.70 × 6.00 mm body, 3.70 × 1.00 mm key, 13.2 × 7.5 mm courtyard, 1.02 mm drills, and an 8.5 mm tall envelope model so renders and STEP exports show the housing. The key is on the top-row side, following the SAO v2 note that the key aligns with pins 1/3/5. The `3V3`, `GND` and `SAO` labels moved outside the housing.

DRC shows no courtyard overlap with the role LEDs on either side. Whether the socket protrudes through the cover is an enclosure decision. Check the key direction with a real add-on before soldering a batch.

## DISP-1: display connector

Three things changed, and the dry-fit is still required.

- **Contact side.** J301 is now a Hirose FH34SRJ-24S-0.5SH(50), which has top and bottom contacts. It takes the same 0.5 mm pitch, 0.3 mm thick flex as the Panasonic AYF532435 the panel maker names, which JLCPCB does not stock. Either face of the flex now makes contact. Source record: [FH34SRJ-24S-0.5SH](../components/FH34SRJ-24S-0.5SH.md).
- **Position.** I had placed the old connector 7.5 mm below the flex. The panel drawing puts the flex end on the glass centreline, so J301 moved from y 145 to y 137.45.
- **Pin order.** The footprint numbers its pads in panel order: pad *n* is Hirose contact 25 − *n*. The component record gives the reasoning from the panel drawing and the fold.

Pin order and flex reach are derived from drawings. A reversed order still means a dead display on every board, so the dry-fit stays the first item before ordering. The edge of the flex is about 0.7 mm from the USB shell; check latch access there too. The footprint and its pad order have had no second-person review.

## UI-1: recovery

- SW403 is a reset button on the left edge, 10 mm above button A, the same Alps part. It pulls EN low through 330 Ω.
- Button B moved from GPIO4 to GPIO0. Hold B and tap reset to enter the ROM bootloader. Silkscreen reads `RST`, `A`, `B/BOOT`.
- GPIO0 keeps its 10 kΩ pull-up, and it is an RTC pin, so B still works as a wake source. Holding B through a power-on or brownout reset also enters the bootloader; reset clears it.

The rear pads remain for a pogo fixture. The battery header J102 is the full power-cycle disconnect; no power switch was added.

Firmware: button B is GPIO0, GPIO4 is free.

## DOC-1: records

- The peripheral contract and implementation record give the TCA9534 start-up as output latch `0x00`, then direction `0x09` (P0 and P3 inputs). The removed PGOOD input and "pulldown input" descriptions are gone.
- `docs/design/display_pinout.md` is generated from the display contract by `tools/write_display_pinout.py`, so it cannot drift again.
- `docs/design/pcb_placement.json` described an older arrangement. It is regenerated from the board by `tools/write_pcb_placement.py`.

## SERVICE-1: references and probes

- 30 reference designators are on the front silkscreen: ICs, connectors, switches, inductors, transistors, diodes and LEDs, wherever a 1.0 mm label fits clear of pads. The other 12 of those classes and the passives stay on the fabrication layer.
- `production/jlcpcb/assembly-front.pdf` and `assembly-rear.pdf` show every reference and are rebuilt with each package.
- Four probe pads join the rear row at y 116: `VSYS`, `EPD` (switched display supply), `SDA`, `SCL`. The row is now ten pads at 3 mm pitch, x 174–201. Battery voltage is at the J102 pins, labelled `BAT + −`.

## SYSTEM-1 and smaller points

- **IR receiver supply.** It now comes from expander P1 (`IR_RX_EN`) instead of the accessory rail. Reception no longer stops on an accessory fault, and firmware can switch the receiver off in storage.
- **Surface finish.** ENIG is better for the dock pads and the 0.5 mm-pitch parts. It is a JLCPCB surcharge, so the release note leaves HASL as the no-cost setting and ENIG as the recommendation. This needs a decision at order time.
- **Charge-complete indication in the dock.** Unchanged. This is a product decision.
- **Qwiic recess, button plungers, optics windows, dock mechanics.** Enclosure work.
- The acceptance tests in the review stand as written.

## Other changes made along the way

- The schematic's power symbols had duplicate references across sheets, which made KiCad report annotation errors on every export. They are renumbered (`tools/renumber_power_symbols.py`); the netlist is unchanged.
- The router's search is now guided by a flood from the target, which let the last three nets route in seconds instead of failing. `tools/pcb_strip_stubs.py` removes dead-end stub chains.
- Rear text moved off the module's thermal vias: organisation under the title, revision and licence along the bottom edge.
- R414 is removed; D101, C303, C507 and C508 moved slightly to make room.

## Before ordering

1. Dry-fit a real panel on a 1:1 print of the front copper or a bare board: pin order, flex reach, latch access.
2. Review the placement and routing in KiCad.
3. Choose the cell and decide HASL or ENIG.
4. Check placement rotations in JLCPCB's preview, in particular J301, the three buttons and the polarised parts.

The [enclosure CAD](../../mechanical/enclosure/README.md) pins the board by hash. Its 30 mechanical references have the same positions, rotations and pads on the final board, so only its `extract_interfaces.py` step needs rerunning.
