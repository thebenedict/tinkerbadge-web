## Power domains and interfaces

- USB_VBUS and DOCK_5V: separate 5 V sources into the priority mux; presence signals are active-low 3.3 V open-drain outputs. USB D+/D− remain USB differential signals.
- VEXT_5V: selected external input. VSYS: charger power-path output. VBAT_PROTECTED: protected ordinary 1S 4.2 V LiPo pack. Battery connector polarity and exact pack/connector must be qualified before connection.
- +3V3: shared nominal 3.3 V supply for logic and the eight RGB LEDs, including storage deep sleep. The user-approved prototype can operate below the LED specified minimum because of tolerance/transients; see the explicit bench qualification gate in implementation_status.md. +3V3_EPD: switched display and isolation-buffer supply. ACC_3V3: current-limited external accessory/IR receiver supply.
- EPD generated analog rails are panel-controller outputs; pin18 VDD is its local 1.8 V output, never the 3.3 V input. See display decisions for bipolar bias rails.
- All MCU GPIO/bus signals are in the 3.3 V domain. RGB_DATA drives the shared-supply LEDs directly; voltage-level analysis does not waive the LED minimum supply boundary. Internal I2C addresses: BH1730FVC 0x29, LIS2DW12 0x19, NTAG 0x55, TCA9534 0x20. External I2C is a separate bus.

## Population and orientation gates

- J102 is a two-wire battery interface: project pin1 positive, pin2 GND. That is a logical assignment, not approval of any vendor cable polarity.
- J104 is an optional, initially unpopulated, separate cell-contact thermistor connection. R120 keeps charger CE high in this initial variant. **Onboard charging is disabled.** A sensor plus a qualified hardware temperature window and charge-current validation are required before enabling it.
- J301 uses the panel flex contact numbering. Its connector footprint is deliberately blank pending exact panel flex, contact-side, fold and mating-view validation.
- J501 follows Qwiic electrical ordering. J502 SAO is a project-assigned host view; exact keyed mating/mirror orientation remains a physical gate. USB shell S1–S4 aliases the one schematic SH node; power-contact shared lands are documented in the power facts.
- DNP is recorded in contract population/value/custom fields where the installed MCP cannot set the native KiCad DNP flag. This is not a manufacturing BOM. Do not infer assembly population from the native flag alone.
- Unused pins below are explicit NCs unless the net says otherwise. Reserved MCU GPIO35–37 are unavailable on N16R8; boot straps, reset pulls, wake and unpowered behavior are in the linked decisions.
