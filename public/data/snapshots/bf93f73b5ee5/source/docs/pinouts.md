## Power domains and interfaces

- USB_VBUS and DOCK_5V: separate 5 V sources into the priority mux; presence signals are active-low 3.3 V open-drain outputs. USB D+/D− remain USB differential signals.
- VEXT_5V: selected external input. VSYS: charger power-path output. VBAT_PROTECTED: protected ordinary 1S 4.2 V LiPo pack. J102 is JST PH, pin 1 positive; check the polarity of each pack type before connection.
- +3V3: shared nominal 3.3 V supply for logic and the eight RGB LEDs, including storage deep sleep. The user-approved prototype can operate below the LED specified minimum because of tolerance/transients; see the explicit bench qualification gate in implementation_status.md. +3V3_EPD: switched display supply. ACC_3V3: current-limited external accessory/IR receiver supply.
- EPD generated analog rails are panel-controller outputs; pin18 VDD is its local 1.8 V output, never the 3.3 V input. See display decisions for bipolar bias rails.
- All MCU GPIO/bus signals are in the 3.3 V domain. RGB_DATA drives the shared-supply LEDs directly; voltage-level analysis does not waive the LED minimum supply boundary. Internal I2C addresses: BH1730FVC 0x29, LIS2DW12 0x19, NTAG 0x55, TCA9534 0x20. External I2C is a separate bus.

## Population and orientation gates

- J102 is a rear through-hole JST PH header: pin 1 battery positive, pin 2 GND. Vendor cable polarity varies; check before connecting.
- Charging is enabled: CE is tied low, RT101 (10 kΩ B3950 NTC in the cell footprint) sets the TS window, R113 sets about 270 mA and the input limit is USB500. Trip temperatures and termination need prototype measurement.
- J301 uses the panel flex contact numbering on a Hirose FH12 bottom-contact footprint. Verify contact side and pin-1 end with a real panel before fabrication.
- J501 follows Qwiic electrical ordering. J502 SAO is a front-mounted host socket: top row 3V3/SDA/GPIO1, bottom row GND/SCL/GPIO2 viewed from the front. USB shell S1–S4 aliases the one schematic SH node; power-contact shared lands are documented in the power facts.
- C508 (NFC trim) carries the native KiCad DNP flag as well as the contract population field. J103 and AE501 are PCB features excluded from the BOM.
- Unused pins below are explicit NCs unless the net says otherwise. Reserved MCU GPIO35–37 are unavailable on N16R8; boot straps, reset pulls, wake and unpowered behavior are in the linked decisions.
